Project Overview

This project explores the design and development of a single-axis gimbal stabilizer. The goal is to create a compact physical platform that senses angular motion and automatically corrects it through active feedback control.

The concept is inspired by aerospace instrument stabilization and control systems, where payloads or sensors must remain properly oriented even when the supporting structure experiences motion or disturbance.

Status: In Development

Objectives

  • Measure platform orientation using the MPU-6050 IMU.
  • Develop an Arduino-based control algorithm.
  • Actuate a servo motor to reduce angular error.
  • Evaluate stabilization accuracy and response behavior.

Minimum Viable Project

Build a working one-axis stabilization platform that keeps a small mounted surface approximately level while its base is manually tilted.

The system must include:

  • A real sensor input
  • A physical actuator output
  • An original feedback control algorithm

Control Strategy

The IMU measures the platform’s angular orientation. The Arduino compares the measured angle to the desired level orientation and commands the servo to reduce the error.

Initial development will use a simple proportional response. Future versions may use a complementary filter and PD/PID control for improved stability.

Risk Evaluation & Mitigation

Potential challenges associated with the development, integration, and testing of the stabilization system.

Sensor Noise and Drift

Inaccurate orientation measurements may reduce stabilization accuracy.

Mitigation: Calibrate the IMU and implement complementary filtering if necessary.

Servo Oscillation

Excessive controller gain may produce oscillations or unstable platform behavior.

Mitigation: Adjust proportional gain and implement an angular deadband if necessary.

Structural Instability

The lightweight cardboard structure may introduce unwanted movement or mechanical deformation.

Mitigation: Reinforce the platform and servo mounting points.

Power Instability

Servo current demand may cause voltage fluctuations or unexpected Arduino resets.

Mitigation: Ensure adequate servo power and a common electrical ground.

Limited Development Time

Advanced control algorithms may require more development and testing than anticipated.

Mitigation: Prioritize a functional minimum viable project before pursuing advanced control algorithms and stretch goals.
